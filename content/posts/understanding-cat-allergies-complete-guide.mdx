Cat allergies affect millions of people worldwide. Understanding how feline allergens operate is the crucial first step toward managing your symptoms effectively.

## What Causes Cat Allergies?
Over 90% of individuals allergic to cats react to **Fel d 1**, a small protein produced in a cat's saliva, skin oil glands, and perianal glands. When cats groom themselves, saliva dries on their fur, flaking off as microscopic airborne dander particles.

## Common Symptoms
* Sneezing and nasal congestion
* Itchy, watery, or red eyes
* Skin rash, hives, or redness where a cat licks
* Coughing or wheezing in sensitive individuals

## How Allergens Travel in the Home
Because Fel d 1 particles are lightweight and sticky, they can remain airborne for hours and attach to upholstered furniture, carpets, clothing, and bedding.

### Path to Relief
Combining HEPA air filtration, routine cleaning, and proper pet grooming can reduce indoor allergen concentrations by up to 80%.
