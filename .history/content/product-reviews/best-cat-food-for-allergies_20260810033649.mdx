## The Science: How Feline Diets Neutralize Allergens

To understand how an allergen-reducing diet functions, it is necessary to examine the underlying biological process of feline allergen distribution:

1. **The Source of Fel d 1:** Cats produce the Fel d 1 protein naturally. It is secreted primarily by their salivary glands and sebaceous (skin) glands. 
2. **The Grooming Transfer:** Cats are meticulous groomers. During this process, salivary proteins coat the fur and skin. As the saliva dries, microscopic allergen particles attach to shed hair and dead skin flakes (dander), which then become airborne and circulate through household air.
3. **Antibody Neutralization:** Over a decade of research led to the discovery that chickens exposed to cats naturally produce an antibody against Fel d 1, which is then concentrated in their eggs. Specialized diets extract this specific egg-derived IgY antibody. When the cat chews the kibble, the antibody binds directly to active Fel d 1 in their saliva, neutralizing up to 47% of the allergen before the cat can groom itself and spread it onto its coat (Purina LiveClear clinical studies).

## How to Safely Transition Your Cat to a New Allergen-Reducing Diet

Feline digestive systems are highly sensitive to sudden dietary changes. To avoid gastrointestinal upset when switching to an allergen-reducing diet like LiveClear, veterinarians recommend a gradual 7-to-10-day transition plan:

* **Days 1–2:** Feed 25% new food mixed with 75% of the current food.
* **Days 3–4:** Feed 50% new food mixed with 50% of the current food.
* **Days 5–6:** Feed 75% new food mixed with 25% of the current food.
* **Day 7 and beyond:** Serve 100% of the new allergen-reducing food.

*Note: For the IgY antibodies to work effectively, the targeted kibble must eventually become the cat's primary, daily source of nutrition.*

Yes. Multi-year clinical safety trials confirm that egg-derived IgY antibodies pass through the feline digestive system safely without altering a cat's baseline metabolism, kidney function, or health status (Purina LiveClear clinical studies).

### How quickly do antibody-based foods show noticeable results?
Reductions in salivary Fel d 1 begin during the first week of feeding. Maximum allergen reductions (averaging up to 47%) are typically measured by the end of week 3 of continuous feeding (Purina LiveClear clinical studies).
