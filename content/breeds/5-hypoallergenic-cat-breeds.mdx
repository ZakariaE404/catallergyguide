Living with cat allergies doesn't automatically mean you can never experience the joy of cat companionship. While no cat is 100% hypoallergenic, certain breeds produce significantly lower levels of the **Fel d 1** protein—the primary allergen produced in feline saliva, skin glands, and dander.

## 1. Siberian Cat
Despite their long, luxurious triple coat, Siberians are famous for producing remarkably low levels of Fel d 1 protein. Many allergy sufferers report mild to zero symptoms around Siberians.

## 2. Balinese
Often called the "Longhaired Siamese," the Balinese is another breed with lower allergen production. Their single-layer coat sheds far less dander into your home environment.

## 3. Bengal
Bengals have a unique, fine pelt-like coat that requires minimal grooming. Because they lick themselves less frequently, less dried saliva-based dander spreads into the air.

## 4. Russian Blue
Russian Blues possess a dense double coat that traps allergens close to their skin rather than releasing dander freely throughout your living space.

## 5. Devon Rex
With short, wavy fur and reduced shedding, Devon Rexes spread fewer airborne dander particles, making routine cleaning and allergy management much easier.

### Essential Takeaway
Before committing to any breed, spend time with an adult cat of that specific breed to test your individual reaction.
