Sleep quality plays a huge role in overall health. If cat dander enters your sleeping quarters, morning congestion and itchy eyes are inevitable.

## Step 1: Encase Mattresses and Pillows
Zip your mattress, box spring, and pillows inside certified allergen-proof covers. These micro-porous barriers prevent dander from penetrating deep into bedding foam.

## Step 2: Run a Dedicated Bedroom HEPA Filter
Keep a quiet HEPA air purifier operating continuously on a low or auto setting right next to your nightstand.

## Step 3: Wash Sheets in Hot Water
Washing bedding once per week in hot water removes shed skin flakes and trapped dust particles.

### Pro Tip
Close bedroom doors during the day so pets don't wander in when you're away!
