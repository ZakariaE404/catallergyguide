When selecting an air purifier specifically for feline allergens, look for high CADR (Clean Air Delivery Rate) ratings and activated carbon filters to absorb pet odors.

## What Makes an Air Purifier Great for Cat Allergies?
* **H13 True HEPA Filter:** Traps tiny dander particles down to 0.1–0.3 microns.
* **Activated Carbon Pre-Filter:** Neutralizes litter box odors and organic smells.
* **High ACH (Air Changes Per Hour):** Cycles room air 4 to 5 times per hour.

## Maintenance Tip
Replace pre-filters every 3 months and primary HEPA filters every 6 to 12 months to maintain peak filtration efficiency.
