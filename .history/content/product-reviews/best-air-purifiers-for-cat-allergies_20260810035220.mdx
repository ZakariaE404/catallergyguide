## The Science of Filtering Cat Allergens

Not all air purifiers are capable of capturing cat allergens. To understand what makes a unit effective, you must look at the physical size of the allergen and the mechanical standards of the filter.

1. **The Size of Fel d 1:** The primary cat allergen is incredibly small, typically measuring between 0.1 and 10 microns in diameter. Because it is so small and sticky, it easily attaches to dust and [cat dander](/allergy-guide/cat-dander-vs-hair-allergies) floating in the air.
2. **True HEPA Standard:** A "True HEPA" filter is certified to capture at least 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns. Because of the physics of airflow (specifically Brownian motion), HEPA filters are actually highly effective at capturing particles even smaller than 0.3 microns, making them perfectly suited for Fel d 1.
3. **Clean Air Delivery Rate (CADR):** This metric indicates the volume of filtered air an air purifier delivers, with separate scores for smoke, pollen, and dust. For cat allergens, the "Smoke" CADR is the most accurate benchmark, as smoke particles share a similar microscopic size range with feline dander.

## Strategic Placement for Maximum Allergen Reduction

To get the most out of your air purifier, placement is just as important as the hardware itself:

1. **Focus on the Bedroom First:** Prioritize clean air where you sleep. Keep the bedroom door closed and run the purifier 24/7 to create an allergen-free sanctuary.
2. **Elevate if Possible:** While floor placement is standard, placing smaller units on a sturdy side table can help capture airborne dander before it settles onto the floor.
3. **Avoid Obstructions:** Ensure there is at least 18 inches of clearance on all sides of the unit to allow for unrestricted airflow.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to run my air purifier 24/7?
Yes. Because cats constantly shed dander and groom themselves, Fel d 1 is continuously introduced into the environment. Running your purifier continuously on an auto or low setting maintains a steady baseline of clean air.

### Are "ionizers" or "ozone generators" safe for cats?
You should strictly avoid ozone generators, as ozone is a lung irritant that is toxic to both humans and felines. Ensure any purifier you buy is certified by the California Air Resources Board (CARB) to emit zero or safely negligible ozone. 

### How often do I need to change the HEPA filter?
For homes with indoor cats, expect to replace the True HEPA filter every 6 to 8 months, depending on the number of cats and the baseline air quality. Never vacuum a HEPA filter, as this damages the micro-fibers and ruins its efficacy; they must be fully replaced.
