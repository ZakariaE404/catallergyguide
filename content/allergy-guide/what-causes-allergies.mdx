Many people assume cat hair causes sneezing, but the scientific culprit is actually a microscopic protein.

## The Fel d 1 Protein
Fel d 1 is an allergen produced exclusively by felines. It is concentrated in saliva and dander, making it easily airborne during grooming.

## How to Manage Exposure
* Install HEPA filters in high-traffic rooms.
* Vacuum regularly using sealed HEPA units.
* Maintain a consistent pet grooming schedule.
