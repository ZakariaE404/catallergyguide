The Balinese cat combines striking sapphire eyes and a silky plume tail with one of the lowest Fel d 1 protein counts in the feline world.

## Why the Balinese Produces Fewer Allergens
It's a common misconception that cat hair triggers allergies. In reality, the culprit is the **Fel d 1** protein secreted in saliva and sebaceous glands. The Balinese naturally secretes much less of this enzyme than non-hypoallergenic breeds.

## Key Personality Traits
* **Affectionate & Vocal:** Like their Siamese ancestors, Balinese cats love engaging in conversational chirps with their humans.
* **Intelligent & Playful:** They thrive on puzzle toys, climbing trees, and interactive games.
* **Low Undercoat:** Lacking a dense undercoat, their long hair lays flat and sheds minimally.

### Care & Grooming Tips
Weekly brushing with a soft slicker brush removes loose hair before it turns into dander, keeping your home environment comfortable and clean.
