## Interpreting Quiz Results
* **Mild Sensitivity:** Environmental controls and periodic dander wipes may be sufficient.
* **Moderate Sensitivity:** Combine bedroom sanctuaries, HEPA filtration, and hypoallergenic cat breeds.
* **Severe Sensitivity:** Consult an allergist regarding immunotherapy (allergy shots) before adopting.
