Siberian cats are renowned for their majestic triple coat and surprisingly low allergen profile.

## Why Siberians Are Great for Allergy Sufferers
Studies have shown that Siberians produce significantly lower concentrations of the Fel d 1 protein in their saliva compared to average domestic cats.

## Temperament and Living Requirements
* Calm, gentle, and dog-like in loyalty.
* Enjoy playing with water and climbing high perches.
* Heavy seasonal shedding requires regular brushing sessions.

### Summary
Siberians offer an ideal option for cat lovers seeking a large, plush companion with low allergen output.
