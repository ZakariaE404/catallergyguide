A widespread myth claims that short-haired or hairless cats cannot trigger allergies. In truth, cat hair itself is not an allergen!

## Dander vs Hair: The Difference
Cat hair acts simply as a vehicle. Microscopic dander—micro-flakes of shed skin impregnated with salivary Fel d 1—clings to individual hairs. When hair sheds, dander hitches a ride into your carpets and furniture.

## Why Hairless Cats Can Still Trigger Symptoms
Sphynx and other hairless breeds still produce saliva and skin oils containing Fel d 1. While they don't shed fur, their skin secretes oils that require regular wiping to prevent allergen buildup.

## Practical Steps to Minimize Exposure
1. Use high-efficiency HEPA air purifiers in key living areas.
2. Wipe down hard surfaces with damp microfiber cloths.
3. Wash your hands thoroughly after petting sessions.
