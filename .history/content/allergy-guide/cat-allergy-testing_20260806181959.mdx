If sneezing, coughing, or itchy eyes flare up whenever a cat is nearby, getting an accurate medical diagnosis is the first step toward long-term relief. 

Many people assume they are allergic to cats when their symptoms are actually triggered by **dust mites, mold, or pollen** that hitch a ride on pet fur. Professional cat allergy testing eliminates the guesswork, helping you pinpoint exact triggers and determine whether feline proteins are truly causing your immune response.

Here is a complete guide to how cat allergy testing works, comparing skin prick tests, blood panels, and at-home diagnostic kits.
