Creating a comfortable home environment with cat allergies requires a multi-layered approach. Here are 10 actionable routines that yield immediate relief.

## 1. Establish an Allergy-Free Sanctuary
Keep your bedroom completely off-limits to cats. Since you spend roughly 8 hours sleeping, an allergen-free bedroom gives your immune system a vital nightly rest.

## 2. Swap Wall-to-Wall Carpeting for Hard Flooring
Carpets trap dander like a giant sponge. Hardwood, tile, or laminate floors can easily be damp-mopped to eliminate settled particles.

## 3. Wash Pet Bedding Weekly
Launder your cat's favorite blankets and beds in hot water (at least 140°F / 60°C) to denature allergen proteins.

## 4. Brush Outside or Near a Filter
Groom your cat regularly using a slicker brush—preferably outdoors or right next to an operating air purifier.
