Equipping your home with targeted pet products can drastically lower allergen levels and keep your family comfortable year-round.

## 1. True HEPA Air Purifiers
Air purifiers equipped with medical-grade True HEPA filters capture 99.97% of airborne particles as small as 0.3 microns, including feline dander and pollen.

## 2. Anti-Allergen Dander Wipes
Pre-moistened conditioning wipes neutralize Fel d 1 proteins on your cat's fur before they dry and become airborne.

## 3. Low-Dust Silica & Paper Cat Litters
Traditional clay litters kick up dusty clouds during scooping. Silica gel crystal litters and dust-free paper pellets keep room air clear.

## 4. Vacuum Cleaners with Sealed HEPA Filtration
Standard vacuums often blow fine dander back into the room. Sealed system HEPA vacuums trap allergens securely inside the bin.
